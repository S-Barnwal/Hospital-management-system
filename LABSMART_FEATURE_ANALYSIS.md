## Positioning
Built for **independent pathology labs and diagnostic centres** (not hospitals). Browser-based (Chrome), 5-day trial + demo, set-up help within 2–3 days, phone/Google Meet support. Plans are priced per year with a **bill quota** (12,000–20,000 bills).

| Plan (pathology) | Price | Adds |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | ₹4,999 + GST / year (≈ ₹417/month) | Registration, billing (pathology, outsource, X-ray, USG, CT, MRI, ECG), panels & packages, business reports, lab reports, QR on bills, SMS/e-mail/WhatsApp reports |
| Advanced | ₹7,499 + GST / year | Multiple user logins, permissions, activity tracking, "internal fraud prevention", browser security, Google review builder |
| Premium | ₹9,999 + GST / year (₹5,999 first year) | Expense management, doctor portal, business analysis, test-count analysis, data export |

Radiology-centre and diagnostic-centre plans add USG / digital X-ray report templates (also ₹4,800/year add-ons). SMS is pay-per-use (20 paise per credit).

## Take-aways for MediCore
1. Labsmart's real strength is **speed and polish of the daily lab desk**: one-screen registration+billing, pre-loaded test database with formulas and age/gender ranges, beautiful configurable PDF, one-click delivery, QR on everything.
2. It is narrower than MediCore: no OPD/IPD/pharmacy/HR/hospital features, no documented sample tracking, no inventory, no critical-value workflow, no multi-level approval.
3. MediCore can **match** it by upgrading the lab reporting engine and report output, and **beat** it with hospital integration, sample tracking, critical-value alerts, two-level approval with amendment history, and true multi-tenant SaaS administration.
